Cebu’s Best Beaches & Islands

From powdery white sands to world-class diving—discover Cebu’s coastal gems with local tips!

Bantayan Island
North Cebu
A classic tropical escape with beaches, biking, seafood, and magical sunsets. Local charm and laid-back vibes await.
  • Sunset at Kota Beach
  • Island hopping: Virgin Island, Paradise Beach
  • Try authentic Cebu dried fish (danggit)
  • Rent a bicycle for town and coast
Tip: Bring cash—ATMs are rare. March–May is best!

Malapascua Island
Daanbantayan, North Cebu
Home to the thresher shark dives, friendly hostels, and coral gardens. Quiet, perfect for beach lovers and scuba divers.
  • Thresher shark diving (Monad Shoal)
  • Snorkeling and coral gardens
  • Island-hopping to Kalanggaman
Tip: Limited ATMs. Most activities run April–July.

Moalboal
South Cebu
Adventure hub famous for snorkeling with sardine runs, turtles, and lively beach bars. Great for solo, family, or group trips.
  • Sardine run snorkeling
  • Turtle watching at Panagsama Beach
  • Canyoneering at nearby Kawasan Falls
  • Chill beach bars, vibrant nightlife
Tip: November–May is the top window. Rent a scooter to explore hidden springs!
